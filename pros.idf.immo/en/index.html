What you need to know

The three questions everyone asks

Am I allowed to do this?

Yes, on one condition: stay in your role. French law reserves the sale of property to licensed professionals. You simply pass on information — that is the role of a business introducer, set out in an agreement we sign together.

Will my name be mentioned?

Only if you decide so. When you share, you choose: either we tell the owner who you are, or we never mention you. You decide, opportunity by opportunity.

What if the sale falls through?

You lose nothing, you pay nothing, you owe nothing. The bonus rewards a sale that actually completes. If the plan goes no further, we tell you and that is the end of it.
